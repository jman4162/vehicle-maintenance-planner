# Annual planning

Review local service history and current Maintenance Minder readings. Forecast only items
supported by manufacturer time fallbacks or an explicit `forecast` state; never invent a
mileage schedule for Maintenance Minder items. Identify stale benchmarks and quotes, refresh
primary-source citations, and prepare user-managed reminders. NHTSA lookup is opt-in and must
state that the VIN will be transmitted.
